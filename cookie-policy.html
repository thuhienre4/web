This Cookie Policy explains how AloCoupon uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They help websites work correctly, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use their services. Similar browser technologies, such as local storage, may serve the same purpose.

How we use cookies and similar technologies

AloCoupon uses a limited number of technologies for the following purposes:

Category Purpose Duration
Strictly necessary Supports security and authenticated access to protected administration features. Up to 8 hours
Preferences Remembers the language you select so the site can use it on your next visit. Until cleared

Third-party websites and affiliate links

Our pages may link to merchants, social networks, or other third-party websites. When you follow one of these links, the third party may set its own cookies under its own policy. AloCoupon does not control cookies placed after you leave our website. Please review the relevant third party's privacy and cookie information before making a purchase or providing personal information.

How to manage cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. You can also clear local site data to remove saved preferences. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent protected parts of the website from working as intended. Your browser's help section provides instructions specific to your device and browser version.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our website or legal obligations change. The latest version will always appear on this page, together with the date it was last updated.
